# 💬 Case Study: Live S2S Conversational AI Evaluation

> **Disclaimer:** *This repository highlights my experience evaluating real-time Speech-to-Speech (S2S) conversational AI models. Due to strict NDAs, specific model names, platforms, and proprietary conversation logs are kept confidential.*

## 📋 Project Overview
Acted as an AI Evaluator for a real-time Live S2S Conversational AI project. The primary goal was to interact with, evaluate, and provide structured feedback on live AI conversations to improve the model's natural responsiveness, context awareness, and conversational flow.

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities & Methodology
*   **Real-Time Interaction Testing:** Conducted live, dynamic conversations with the AI to test its ability to handle dynamic dialogue, complex queries, and conversational nuances.
*   **Latency & Turn-Taking Evaluation:** Assessed the model's response time (latency), interruption handling, and natural turn-taking capabilities to ensure a seamless human-like interaction.
*   **Context Retention & Reasoning:** Evaluated the AI's ability to maintain context over multi-turn conversations and provide logically consistent, safe, and accurate responses.
*   **Linguistic & Audio Quality:** Monitored the model's generated audio for proper prosody, emotion, and clarity during live interactions.
*   **Structured Feedback & Annotation:** Provided detailed qualitative and quantitative feedback into the data annotation platform to help ML engineering teams identify failure points and hallucination triggers.

## 🚀 Impact
*   Helped identify critical edge cases in real-time conversational flows, directly contributing to reducing model latency and improving context retention.
*   Ensured the continuous improvement of the AI's natural language understanding (NLU) and generation (NLG) in live scenarios.
